Let's call this what it actually is:

Your 401(k) match is **part of your paycheck**.

Not a bonus. Not a perk. Not a "nice-to-have if I remember."

It's compensation, whether you work the cabin or the ramp.

And if you're not taking it, you're voluntarily giving some of your pay back to the company.

## ✈️ What a 401(k) Match Really Means

When your employer says:

> "We'll match X% of your contributions..."

They're saying:

> "If you invest, we'll pay you more."

On the money you put in to earn it, that match is an immediate return before your investments do anything at all. If your plan matches dollar-for-dollar, that's a 100% instant return, which is hard to beat anywhere. (Check your plan's details, including any vesting schedule.)

## 🧠 Why People Skip the Match (And Why It's Costly)

Common reasons:

- "I'll start later"
- "I need the cash right now"
- "I don't understand the investments"

All understandable.

But skipping the match can add up to a lot of money over a career.

Not because of bad investing. Because of **missed free money**.

## ✈️ The Order of Operations (This Matters)

If you remember nothing else, remember this:

**Get the full match before anything else.**

Before:

- IRAs
- Brokerage accounts
- Extra savings

The match is step one. (Last week's post, [Where to Put Your Money First](https://coachinflight.com/blog/where-to-put-your-money-first), walks through what comes after it.)

## 📊 What This Looks Like Over Time

A small match early in your career:

- Compounds for decades
- Grows tax-deferred
- Becomes a meaningful chunk of retirement

It's quiet. It's boring. It's powerful.

## 🧳 "But I Might Leave This Job..."

Your vested balance:

- Is still yours
- Can be rolled over later
- Moves with you

Leaving it behind doesn't help you. Taking it with you does.

## 🛬 Final Thoughts: Take the Raise

You don't have to max your 401(k). You don't have to love investing. You don't have to understand every option.

You just have to **accept the raise**.

The match is one of the easiest financial wins you'll ever get. Don't leave it on the table.

If you're unsure whether you're getting your full match, check. This is one of those small decisions that quietly changes everything.
